A Blip Adventure

The Shark
Adventure

Blip had landed somewhere new. There was water. A lot of water.

Scroll to begin ↓

Blip stared at the ocean.

The ocean did not appear particularly interested in Blip.

This seemed rude.

Blip shuffled a little closer.

A wave rolled in.

Blip shuffled back.

Another wave rolled in.

Blip considered this.

STORY IMAGE 01

Blip arriving at the beach

There were probably several sensible ways to explore the sea.

A boat, for example.

Swimming lessons would also have been useful.

Blip had neither of those things.

What Blip did have was confidence.

Possibly too much confidence.

Then, half buried in the sand, Blip spotted something blue.

It had a fin.

It had teeth.

Lots of teeth.

Blip picked it up.

This was either a shark costume...

...or a very concerning hat.

STORY IMAGE 02

Blip discovers the shark costume

Blip put it on.

Naturally.

The transformation was immediate.

Blip now had a fin.

A tail.

And enough teeth to make several very poor decisions.

STORY IMAGE 03

Full Shark Blip reveal

Blip looked toward the ocean again.

The ocean still looked suspiciously large.

But now?

Now Blip looked like a shark.

Sort of.

Blip waddled toward the water.

One step.

Two steps.

Three—

Something moved beneath the surface.

Blip froze.

The water rippled.

A dark shape passed beneath the waves.

Blip slowly looked down at the shark costume.

Then back at the ocean.

Then back at the shark costume.

This may not have been the disguise Blip thought it was.

STORY IMAGE 04

A fin approaches Blip

Another fin broke the surface.

Blip stood very still.

The fin came closer.

And closer.

And closer.

Blip raised one tiny arm.

The fin stopped.

For one strange moment, everything was quiet.

Then the fin turned.

And disappeared.

Blip waited.

Nothing happened.

Blip looked extremely pleased with this result.

Apparently, this counted as a successful shark encounter.

Blip strutted along the beach in the shark suit for the rest of the afternoon.

No one corrected this version of events.

Probably for the best.

STORY IMAGE 05

Blip watching the ocean at sunset

As the sun began to drop, Blip looked back over the water.

Far beyond the shore, something moved again.

Bigger this time.

Much bigger.

Blip tilted their head. Somewhere out there... the ocean was keeping secrets. And Blip had absolutely no intention of leaving them alone.

End of this adventure

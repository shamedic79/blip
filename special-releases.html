Kickstarter

Where Blip began.

Blip's first big adventure was Kickstarter.

The campaign was created to help kick Blip's existence off and bring the first collection of enamel pins into the real world. Alongside the original releases came special green Kickstarter colourways made to mark the very beginning of the journey.

These releases represent the starting point — the moment Blip went from an idea to something collectors could actually hold.

21–23 August 2026

Singapore

Blip goes international.

In August 2026, Blip packed his bags for Singapore as Loot in a Boot made its Singapore debut at POP TOY SHOW Singapore.

To celebrate the occasion, two Blip pins were created especially around the Singapore adventure: Blipion, with Blip dressed in his own Merlion-inspired disguise, and Blip in Singapore, capturing the little traveller surrounded by some unmistakably Singaporean sights.

The release celebrated Loot in a Boot launching in Singapore alongside our friends at Otakaraya, who hosted Blip and R.K. at their POP TOY SHOW 2026 booth.
